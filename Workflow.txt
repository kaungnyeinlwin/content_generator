Workflow
=========
1. Idea

2. Clarify Requirements
- users
- goals
- constraints
- MVP

3. Discuss with AI

4. Confirm Product Requirements

5. Generate HLD

6. Review HLD manually and add the HLD in project folder

7. Break HLD into milestones

8. Generate implementation tasks

9. Start agentic coding
- one milestone at a time

10. Review every pull/request or feature
- test
- refactor
- commit

11. Repeat (Human in the loop)
